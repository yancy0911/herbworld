# HerbWorld Manhattan Pilot: Legal and Safety Research

Research date: 2026-06-12

This is an operational risk summary, not legal advice. Counsel should review the model before adding payments, insurance, donations, stored value, delivery workers, or nationwide operations.

## Current low-risk launch model

- Users offer reusable household items for free.
- HerbWorld reviews listings and coordinates introductions.
- HerbWorld does not own, store, inspect professionally, transport, or accept payment for items.
- Paid services are provided by separately verified businesses under written quotes.
- No user-to-user payment, escrow, cash pool, insurance, donation, or contribution-credit feature is active.

## Product safety

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission states that its laws and regulations apply to anyone who sells or distributes consumer products, including charities and individuals holding yard sales or flea markets. Used products may be recalled or dangerous. The pilot therefore requires manual approval, CPSC recall review, prohibited-product rules, and immediate removal of unsafe reports.

Official source:
https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/ResaleThrift-Stores-Information-Center

Recall database:
https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls

## Moving businesses

NYSDOT states that consumers moving within New York should use a carrier licensed by the Commissioner of Transportation. Interstate movers must be registered with the federal government and have a U.S. DOT number. The pilot therefore must verify applicable authority before referring moving work.

Official sources:
https://www.dot.ny.gov/divisions/operating/osss/truck/moving
https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/search-mover

## Data security

The New York SHIELD Act requires businesses maintaining covered private information to maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. The pilot minimizes public data, protects operations data, records consent versions, applies rate limits, and maintains an incident-response procedure.

Official source:
https://ag.ny.gov/resources/organizations/data-breach-reporting/shield-act

## NYC business licensing

NYC DCWP licenses businesses in regulated industries. Each partner must be checked for licenses applicable to its actual work. HerbWorld should not state that a business is verified until the relevant license and insurance have been checked.

Official source:
https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/businesses/licenses.page

## Features requiring a new legal review

- Charging users or sellers transaction fees.
- Processing or holding payments.
- Paid listings or sales of goods.
- Employing or dispatching individual workers.
- Delivery-app functionality.
- Donations, mutual aid, insurance, cash pools, stored value, or contribution credits.
- Cross-border shipping or customs activity.
- Expansion outside the initial local pilot.
