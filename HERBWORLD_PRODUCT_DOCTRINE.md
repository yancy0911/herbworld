# HerbWorld Share Product Doctrine

Updated: 2026-06-12

## Mission

Make it easier to reuse a useful object than to throw it away.

HerbWorld Share begins as a nearby free-item network. A person publishes an item they no longer need, another nearby person requests it, and the handoff is verified with a one-time code. The product records a completed reuse event only after the physical handoff happens.

## The Product Stack

### Layer 1: Utility

The first screen must solve a real problem in seconds:

- See useful free items nearby.
- Publish an unwanted item quickly.
- Request an item without exposing private contact details publicly.
- Confirm the handoff with a one-time code.
- Report unsafe, misleading, recalled, or prohibited items.

This layer must remain usable without payment, referrals, walking targets, or game progress.

### Layer 2: Trust

Trust is the core product, not a support function:

- Human moderation before public listing.
- Approximate location before acceptance; minimum necessary handoff details afterward.
- One-time handoff code that expires and cannot be reused.
- Clear prohibited-item and recall-review process.
- Publisher blocking, listing hiding, reporting, audit records, and incident response.
- Verified reuse events instead of unverified clicks or self-reported claims.

### Layer 3: Impact

After a verified handoff, both participants can see the real-world result:

- Item successfully reused.
- Estimated waste avoided, shown only when a defensible category model exists.
- Distance and transport mode, only when voluntarily supplied.
- Neighborhood and city reuse totals using aggregated data.

Impact estimates must be labeled as estimates and must never exaggerate environmental benefit.

### Layer 4: Movement

Movement supports participation but never gates the core service:

- Users may voluntarily connect Apple Health to convert walking progress into non-cash game energy.
- Health and fitness data remains on-device whenever possible.
- Health data is never sold, used for advertising, or added to AI-training datasets.
- Walking rewards cannot be redeemed for cash, insurance, regulated benefits, or ownership of real goods.

### Layer 5: World

The original HerbWorld game becomes the emotional and cultural layer:

- Verified reuse restores a user's local world.
- Walking supplies energy to explore and care for the world.
- Cities open after local operations, moderation, and safety systems are ready.
- Historical scenes, cultural collection, and global collaboration arrive only after the reuse network works reliably.

The game visualizes real participation. It must not manufacture environmental claims or distract users from completing real handoffs.

## Defensible Data Asset

The valuable dataset is not a collection of private user information or generic images. It is a permissioned, auditable graph of real-world reuse:

- Object category and structured condition.
- Moderated images with sensitive details removed.
- Approximate geography.
- Publication, request, acceptance, and verified handoff events.
- Time-to-reuse and final outcome.
- Optional transport and repair information.

Every data field must have a declared purpose, retention rule, access rule, and deletion behavior. Personal contact details, precise addresses, private messages, and HealthKit data are excluded from data licensing and AI-training datasets.

## Global Architecture

The product is global by design but opens city by city.

Each activated city requires:

- Local prohibited-item policy and legal review.
- Moderation coverage and escalation contact.
- Local language and emergency guidance.
- Safe handoff guidance and partner verification process.
- Measured response times and incident handling.

Users may browse other places later, but an inactive city must not pretend to offer local operational support.

## Version Sequence

### Version 1: Verified Local Reuse

- Manhattan pilot.
- Free publishing and claiming.
- Human moderation.
- One-time handoff code.
- Reports, blocks, safety pages, and protected operations dashboard.

### Version 1.5: Accounts and Reuse History

- Sign in with Apple.
- Account deletion and data export.
- Publisher acceptance of a claim.
- Secure in-app handoff details.
- Verified reuse history and basic impact receipts.

### Version 2: Nearby Discovery

- Permission-based approximate location.
- Distance bands instead of public exact location.
- Saved searches and notifications.
- Multilingual content and city configuration.

### Version 3: Movement and World

- Optional HealthKit integration.
- On-device movement progress.
- Non-cash game energy.
- Local world restoration tied to verified reuse.

### Version 4: Responsible Data Products

- Separate, explicit opt-in for eligible reuse data.
- Provenance and consent ledger.
- De-identification and sensitive-image review.
- External data access only under contracts, audits, and deletion obligations.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Core free reuse is never locked behind referrals, payment, steps, or game progress.
- Never describe unverified clicks as completed reuse.
- Never sell personal data.
- Never use HealthKit data for advertising, data mining, or AI training.
- Never expose full home addresses publicly.
- Never launch a city without operational safety coverage.
- Never add payments, stored value, insurance, donations, or cash-like rewards without a separate legal review.
