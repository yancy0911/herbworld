# HerbWorld Manhattan Pilot Launch Operations SOP

Version: 2026-06-12

## Launch scope

- Manhattan only during the initial pilot.
- Free reusable household items only.
- No platform payments, deposits, auctions, delivery custody, storage, or guarantees.
- Paid moving, cleaning, assembly, and removal are performed by independent businesses after written quotes.

## Before approving an item

1. Confirm the publisher owns the item and is offering it completely free.
2. Review photos, condition, defects, dimensions, missing parts, stairs, elevator, and lifting risks.
3. Search the brand and model in the CPSC recall database and review current safety warnings.
4. Reject prohibited, recalled, unstable, incomplete, damaged, or safety-critical products.
5. Confirm public text contains no contact information, full address, precise location, or sensitive data.
6. Complete all four checks in `/operations` before approving.

## Before matching a service business

1. Verify legal business name, contact, service area, and current insurance.
2. For New York intrastate household-goods moving, verify the NYSDOT authority directly with NYSDOT.
3. For interstate moving, verify applicable FMCSA authority.
4. Obtain a written quote with scope, total price, cancellation terms, timing, and responsibility for damage.
5. Send the user the business identity and quote before work begins.
6. Do not collect or hold user payment during the pilot.

## Safe handoff

- Recommend daylight and a public or monitored handoff location.
- Do not publish or share a full address until both parties are confirmed.
- Do not require either party to enter a stranger's home.
- The recipient inspects and accepts the item directly from the publisher.
- If anyone reports danger, harassment, a recalled product, or fraud, unpublish immediately and preserve relevant records.

## Daily operations

- Check `/operations` at least twice daily.
- Review new items before they become public.
- Contact claimants in submission order, subject to safety and feasibility.
- Mark unavailable items promptly.
- Review service requests and only send them to verified businesses.
- Record incidents, complaints, and resolution steps.

## Incident response

1. Remove affected public content.
2. Restrict access to relevant records.
3. Identify affected people and information.
4. Preserve logs and document decisions.
5. Notify affected users and authorities when legally required.
6. Correct the control failure before restoring the affected workflow.

## Do not launch these features yet

- Cash pools, mutual aid, insurance, donations, stored value, contribution credits, or escrow.
- User-to-user payments.
- Cross-border shipping or customs services.
- Unverified movers or businesses.
- Automated approval of public items.
