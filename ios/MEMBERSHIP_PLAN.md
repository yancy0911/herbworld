# Membership and Referral Plan

## Version 1.0

- Free download for everyone.
- Free publishing and free claiming remain available to everyone.
- First 100 verified real users are recorded as Founding Members after an account system is added.
- No payment or referral gate in version 1.0.

## Later version

- Founding Members receive permanent recognition and selected premium conveniences.
- A verified referral can grant a limited premium period.
- Users who do not refer can buy the same premium period through Apple In-App Purchase.
- Premium may include faster review, saved-search alerts, and access to special partner quotes.
- Core free reuse features remain available without referrals or payment.

This avoids forcing users to perform marketing activity to access core functionality and keeps digital membership purchases within Apple's In-App Purchase system.
