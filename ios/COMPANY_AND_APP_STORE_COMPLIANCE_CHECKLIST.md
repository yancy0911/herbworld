## New York Business Compliance Review

Polaris Global L.L.C. is a Minnesota entity while the pilot is operated from Manhattan. Before enabling paid New York services or representing the company as authorized in New York:

- Keep the New York sales-tax Certificate of Authority and its filing obligations separate from foreign-LLC authority. The sales-tax registration does not itself establish authority to conduct business as a foreign LLC.
- Confirm the formal New York sales-tax Certificate of Authority has been approved and received, then calendar every required sales-tax filing even during periods with no taxable sales.
- Ask a New York business attorney or CPA whether current activities constitute doing business in New York.
- If required, file New York `Application for Authority` for a foreign LLC.
- Obtain a Minnesota certificate of existence/good standing acceptable to New York.
- Complete New York's LLC publication requirement within the applicable deadline after authority is granted.
- Confirm New York tax registrations and sales-tax treatment before charging for taxable services.
